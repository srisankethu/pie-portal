# How you get paid

One page. If you cannot work out your own number from this, tell us — an
incentive you cannot compute is not an incentive, and that would be our bug.

## The one number: Contribution Above Floor

Every line you sell earns:

> **(your price − the published floor) × quantity**
> **− anything paid to the customer's people**
> **− half of what you spend on trials, studies and training**
> **+ every rupee you get out of the vendor**

The floor is published on every item. You can see it. You can add this up
yourself before you quote.

## Four things that follow from it

**A discount is your money.** ₹10 off a 500-piece line is ₹5,000 out of your
own points — the same whether you call it a discount, freight, packing or free
credit. There is no wrapper that hides it.

**A rupee from the vendor is worth exactly as much as a rupee held on price.**
Get project pricing, a quantity break, free tooling, training, demo stock —
send the document and it counts, one for one. Asking is worth as much as
holding.

**Money to the customer's people costs you 100 paise. A trial or a study costs
you 50.** That is deliberate. The legitimate tool is half price.

**On a PSU, government or defence account, the incentive field will not save.**
Not a policy, not an approval — the record cannot be created. The law reaches
the giver, and a GeM blacklisting ends that business permanently.

## You earn it when the money arrives

| They pay | You get |
|---|---|
| In advance | **110%** |
| On the due date | 100% |
| Up to 30 days late | 90% |
| 31–60 | 75% |
| 61–90 | 50% |
| 91–120 | 25% |
| Beyond 120 | **nothing, and anything already advanced comes back** |

Chasing the payment is not an extra task. It is when you get paid.

## Which accounts are worth more

| Account | Base | Growth |
|---|---|---|
| New | — (all of it is growth) | **1.80×** |
| Developing | 1.10× | 1.60× |
| Established | 0.95× | 1.50× |
| Anchor | 0.85× | **1.60×** |

Anchors pay 1.60× on growth on purpose — growing inside a big account is hard
and it is worth a lot. But **let an established account fall more than 20% and
every growth multiplier you have drops to 1.00 for the period.** You cannot
fund hunting by letting the base rot.

## Three things that hold the whole period

Fail any of them and the period is held — not lost. Fix it within 60 days and
it is released.

1. Every customer incentive declared, before invoicing. None on PSU accounts.
2. No unmastered items, HSN present, customer PO on the order.
3. Nothing past 120 days overdue without a written recovery plan.

## When you get it

Points update daily and you can see them. Cash is quarterly at **70%**. The
other **30%** sits in your Relationship Bank for a year and is released then —
so a customer who disputes, churns or never pays reduces a balance you can
already see.

## Clearing old stock

Old stock has a lower floor, and clearing it pays a separate bounty on top —
open to anyone in the company who spots the match, split 40/60 between whoever
proposed it and whoever closed it. If it was your order that created the stock,
you earn a quarter rate on that particular stock.

## Working it out before you agree it

The **Negotiation desk** in the portal does the arithmetic above while you are
on the phone. Put in the item, the quantity and the price you are about to
agree, and it gives you the floor, what the line contributes, what a discount
costs, what the vendor ask is worth, and the price you would have to hold to
keep a number you have in mind. Move any figure and it moves.

Nothing there is hidden from you and nothing there is a different sum from the
one above — it is the same calculator that pays you at the end of the month, so
if you disagree with the desk, one of us has made an arithmetic mistake and it
can be found.

Two things the desk will refuse:

- **An item we have no purchase record for has no floor**, so it computes
  nothing rather than treating the whole price as contribution. Get the item
  mastered.
- **A payment to someone at a customer that has not been classified as a
  private account.** Unclassified is treated as government until an owner says
  otherwise. Trials and training are open on every account.

## The short version

Hold your price. Get the vendor to pay for what the customer wants. Use a trial
instead of cash. Make sure the money arrives. Do not let a good account slide.

Nobody has to check up on you for any of it — the arithmetic already does.
